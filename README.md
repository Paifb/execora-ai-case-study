# EXECORA — AI Solutions Engineering

### Intelligent Agents | Business Automation | Decision Intelligence

**Technical Case Study — Fabiano Silva | AI Solutions Engineer**

## Overview

EXECORA is an evolving ecosystem of intelligent business solutions designed to connect artificial intelligence, software engineering, data and business operations.

The project explores how specialized AI agents and human oversight can support research, analysis, automation and business decision-making.

## Business Challenge

Organizations often work with fragmented information, repetitive processes and disconnected software tools. EXECORA aims to bring these workflows into a coordinated environment with clearer visibility, traceability and human control.

## Solution Architecture

The platform's documented design includes:

- **Command Center:** Interface for managing business workflows and reviewing information.
- **ORACLE:** Mission creation and coordination.
- **SCOUT:** AI-assisted business research using public sources.
- **ARCHITECT:** Preparation of business diagnostics and proposed approaches.
- **Approval Gate:** Human review before external outreach.
- **Business Memory:** Recording decisions, reviews and outcomes for future reference.

## Engineering Focus

- AI-assisted workflows and specialized agents
- API integrations and backend development
- Human-in-the-loop governance
- Business data organization and persistence
- Auditability and operational visibility
